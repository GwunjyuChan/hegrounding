Jena 134, Auerstadt 27. **Isserstedt is spelled "Isserstadt"** (28 hits). An alternative scan with a full title page exists, but it is the 1914 reissue (`in.ernet.dli.2015.66350`, "MCMXIV"), so I did not use it.
- **petre1912_germany**: Kulm 45, Vandamme 118, Leipzig 288; "Vandamme destroyed at Kulm" in the contents.
- **sargent1897_marengo**: "A. C. McClurg and Company … 1897"; Castel-Ceriolo 6, Victor 101.
- **bergen_annualregister1814**: "for the year 1814 … 1815". Graham's dispatch "From Sir T. Graham.—Failure at Bergen-op-Zoom" (p. 169 of the Chronicle/State Papers; text ~lines 42570–43020). It names Skerrett, Gore, Carleton, Morrice and Cooke.

- **clausewitz1833_1799_bd5**: "Fünfter Band … Die Feldzüge von 1799 in Italien und der Schweiz. Erster Theil … 1833". Section "Erste Schlacht bei Zürich den 4. Juni" at ~line 14978; Hotze 100, Bey 6. The Fraktur OCR writes Petrasch as "Petraſch" (long s), so a plain grep for "Petrasch" finds nothing. Search for `Petra.ch`.
